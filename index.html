Executive 0: What We Heard

Duration: 0:30 | Cumulative: 0:30
DO

Open "What we heard" slide and quickly call out: fragmented systems, manual intake, manual routing, closure visibility gap.

Before we start the product tour, here is the reality your team is living today. Intake is fragmented across phone, walk-in, letter, email, social, and COM-WEB with no single intake point. Agents must manually enter feedback into INFOCOM, manually classify subtype and ADA relevance, and manually route to the right SME using static lists and block-to-division references.

After assignment, teams still carry manual burden: the 24-hour assignment window is not system-enforced, routing depends on personal knowledge instead of skills-based matching, and SMEs must piece together investigation data across INFOCOM, Swiftly, Transit Master/OnRoute, and cut reports. Evidence collection is manual, operator interviews are hard to coordinate, customer updates are not automated, and closure has no structured audit trail. Reporting comes after-the-fact through manual manager reports rather than live visibility.

Our objective in this demo is to show how Maryland MTA can move from that manual model to one connected process with faster resolution, clearer ownership, and stronger rider trust.

30-second version: Today, OCCR work is fragmented, manual, and hard to track from intake through closure. Agents and SMEs re-enter data, route cases manually, gather evidence through separate processes, and report outcomes after the fact. In this demo, we show one connected process that unifies intake, improves routing and investigation quality, automates customer communication, and gives leadership live visibility.
